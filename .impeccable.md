## Design Context

### Users
International classical-music listeners looking for a quick, consolidated view of upcoming concerts by date, country, city, source, and composer.

### Brand Personality
ClassicalBot is restrained, elegant, and useful. The design should feel calm and direct.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve the light editorial design: Playfair Display headings, neutral typography, simple tables, subtle blue links, and generous whitespace. Avoid ornamental redesigns, dense dashboards, and card-heavy layouts.

### Design Principles
- Keep concert discovery faster than browsing individual venue sites.
- Let programme data and typography provide the hierarchy.
- Preserve clarity and touch-friendly behavior on mobile.
- Use decoration only when it communicates state or interaction.
